A SMALL GUIDE TO A BIG INTERNET

There’s more to the internet than the same five tabs.

INFINITELY is an independently curated directory for people who want a useful distraction, a playful experiment, a small challenge, or simply something unexpected to explore.

The web is full of projects made by curious people: tiny games, interactive explanations, sound experiments, visual toys, puzzles, and places to learn something in a few minutes. We collect a selection of those destinations in one easy-to-browse place.

What you’ll find here

Recommendations are grouped by the kind of experience they offer. Mini games are quick to start. Visual toys invite you to experiment. Brain-training picks offer a puzzle or a challenge. Weird-and-wonderful links are for the unexpected, while chill and learning picks are better suited to a slower visit.

Use search when you have something in mind, choose a category to narrow the list, or use the surprise button when you would rather let chance choose your next detour. The save control stores selected recommendation names in your current browser so you can find them again on that device.

How recommendations are selected

We look for destinations that offer a clear experience, are understandable from their landing page, and add something distinct to the collection. A recommendation should be more than a generic link: its description should tell you what you can do there and why you might enjoy it.

We also consider practical details such as whether a destination appears to work without an account, whether it has obvious age or privacy considerations, and whether its content may surprise visitors. Where a relevant caution is known, we try to mention it in the listing. This is a lightweight editorial review, not a guarantee of safety or an endorsement by the destination.

Independent websites, independent rules

INFINITELY does not own or operate the websites listed here. Their operators control their own content, availability, cookies, privacy practices, account requirements, and terms. A destination can change after we add it, so check its own information before signing up, sharing data, downloading anything, or making a purchase.

A sensible browsing rule: open unfamiliar links thoughtfully, keep personal and payment information private unless you trust the service, and leave any page that behaves unexpectedly.

Keeping the directory useful

Links can move, services can change, and a once-interesting page can disappear. We may update or remove recommendations when we discover a broken destination, a material change, or a concern that makes it a poor fit. If you spot a broken link or want to flag a concern, email midnightarchive222@gmail.com.

Who runs INFINITELY?

INFINITELY is an independent web project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially connected to the third-party services it lists.
