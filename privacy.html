Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

INFINITELY is a small discovery website that lists links to independently operated websites. This policy explains the limited information-handling practices of this website.

Information stored in your browser

The Saved for later feature stores the names of your saved recommendations in your browser's local storage. This information stays in that browser on that device unless you clear the site's data. INFINITELY does not provide an account or sync saved items between devices.

Information collected by INFINITELY

The current static version of INFINITELY does not ask you to register, does not provide forms for personal information, and does not intentionally operate analytics, advertising trackers, or its own user database.

Third-party services

The website loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google's servers to retrieve those font files. When you follow an external recommendation, you leave INFINITELY and interact with a separate website. That website may collect information such as your IP address, device or browser information, cookies, or information you choose to submit. Its own privacy policy and terms apply.

Your choices

  • You can clear local storage or site data in your browser to remove saved recommendations.
  • You can block third-party cookies or external resources using your browser settings, although some features or styling may change.
  • You can choose not to open external links or provide information to third-party websites.

Children and sensitive information

INFINITELY is not designed to collect personal information from children. Some linked services have their own age restrictions. Review their rules before use, and do not submit sensitive personal information to external services unless you understand how it will be used.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or services change. The revision date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

A public contact address has not yet been configured for this website. A working contact method should be added before commercial launch.
