What happens when a link changes?

If a destination is confirmed broken, redirects to an unrelated or unsafe page, changes its core experience, or introduces a material concern, we will update its description, add a caution, replace it, or remove it. A temporary outage or crawler restriction alone is not enough evidence to remove a recommendation.

Report a problem at midnightarchive222@gmail.com. Include the destination name and what happened.
